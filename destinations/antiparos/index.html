The destination

Getting married in Antiparos

Antiparos is a short crossing from Paros: a small, discreet island of private villas, quiet beaches and a laid-back village.

It is ideal for couples who want privacy and a relaxed atmosphere for their guests.

Getting there
A short crossing from Paros.
Season
May to October
Ideal for
Private, relaxed celebrations.
