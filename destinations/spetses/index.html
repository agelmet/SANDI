The destination

Getting married in Spetses

Spetses combines neoclassical mansions, horse-drawn carriages and pine-fringed coves with an aristocratic charm, close to Athens.

It is a graceful setting for classic weddings and wedding weekends.

Getting there
By sea from Piraeus.
Season
May to October
Ideal for
Classic, graceful weddings.

I Wedding inspiration

  • A ceremony in a mansion garden
  • A seaside reception at sunset
  • Guests touring the island by carriage
  • A farewell lunch by the old harbour

II Suggested venues

  • Historic mansions
  • Hotels
  • Seaside venues
