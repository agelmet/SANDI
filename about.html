About

An event is remembered for how it made people feel.

Sandi Events is the event design house of Kyriaki Kalou, based in Athens and working all over Greece.

The house

A clear idea, flawless aesthetics, an organisation nobody notices.

Sandi Events is the event design house of Kyriaki Kalou. We create corporate events, brand launches and private celebrations for companies and people who expect much more than a well-run evening: a clear idea, flawless aesthetics and an organisation that nobody notices — because everything simply works.

Every event starts from a conversation: who you are, who your guests are and what they should take home. Then we take on everything — the concept and the design, the venue, the suppliers, the timing and the coordination on the day — with one person responsible from the first call to the last guest leaving.

One person responsible, from the first call to the last guest leaving.

How we work

From the first call to the last guest.

  1. 01

    Getting to know you

    A call or a meeting: the occasion, the guests, the date, the budget and what you dream of.

  2. 02

    Concept & mood board

    We come back with the idea of the event: aesthetics, palette, atmosphere and venue options.

  3. 03

    Proposal & budget

    A clear written proposal with every cost, so you decide calmly and with no surprises.

  4. 04

    Production

    Suppliers booked, design finalised, run sheet written — and regular updates for you.

  5. 05

    The day itself

    We are there from set-up to the end, directing every moment, so you only welcome your guests.

  6. 06

    Afterwards

    Supplier payments closed, photos and thank-you notes — and an event your guests will talk about.
