Religious & Multicultural Weddings in Greece

SANDI MOMENTS · 25.11.2026

  1. Home
  2. Journal
  3. Religious & Multicultural Weddings in Greece

At SANDI MOMENTS, we believe every love story deserves to be celebrated. Greece welcomes couples of every faith, culture and background — and every tradition deserves the same care.

Every faith, culture and tradition

We coordinate weddings of different religious and cultural traditions, including Greek Orthodox, Catholic, Protestant, Anglican, Jewish, Muslim, Hindu and Buddhist weddings, as well as interfaith and multicultural celebrations.

Civil and symbolic ceremonies

Many couples choose a civil ceremony, or a symbolic ceremony that can take place almost anywhere — on a beach, in a garden or on a terrace above the sea. Others combine a legal ceremony at home with a symbolic celebration in Greece.

Working with the right people

We work closely with couples and the appropriate religious representatives to create a meaningful celebration. Legal and religious procedures always follow the requirements of the relevant authorities and representatives, and we guide you through the documentation step by step.

One celebration, many traditions

For multicultural weddings, we help you bring both families' traditions together — in the ceremony, the music, the menu and the details — so that everyone feels at home.
