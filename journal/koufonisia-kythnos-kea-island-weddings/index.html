Koufonisia, Kythnos & Kea: Three Cycladic Islands for an Intimate Wedding

SANDI MOMENTS · 25.11.2026

  1. Home
  2. Journal
  3. Koufonisia, Kythnos & Kea: Three Cycladic Islands for an Intimate Wedding

Santorini and Mykonos are famous for good reason. But some of the most beautiful weddings in Greece happen on smaller islands, where the setting feels personal and the guests become part of the island for a few days.

Koufonisia: turquoise water and a barefoot rhythm

In the Lesser Cyclades, between Naxos and Amorgos, Koufonisia are known for some of the clearest water in the Aegean and a single, whitewashed harbour village. Everything is close: guests meet on the same lanes and beaches, and a wedding weekend quickly feels like a gathering of friends.

Koufonisia suit intimate weddings and elopements — a ceremony by the sea at golden hour, a long table under the stars, a boat day for the guests.

Kythnos: authentic and close to Athens

Kythnos is reached by ferry from Lavrio or Piraeus, which makes it one of the easiest true Cycladic settings for guests travelling from Athens. Its stone villages, chapels on the hills and the double bay of Kolona give it an authentic character that has not changed much over the years.

Kea: elegant and understated

Kea, also called Tzia, is the Cycladic island closest to Athens — about an hour by ferry from Lavrio. The amphitheatrical village of Ioulida, terraced hills and private villas make it a refined choice for a wedding weekend, especially for international guests flying into Athens.

How to choose

Think about your guest list, the atmosphere you want and how your guests will travel. Smaller islands have fewer venues and fewer rooms, so early planning matters: we recommend starting 12–18 months before the wedding, especially for summer dates.

We are happy to walk you through each island, its venues and the logistics for your guests.
