FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything couples, companies and private clients ask us most often — from timings and seasons to destination weddings and private dining.

01What types of corporate events does SANDI MOMENTS organise in Athens and Greece?+

SANDI MOMENTS provides corporate event planning services for conferences, gala dinners, award ceremonies, business meetings, product launches, brand activations, networking events, company celebrations and incentive events. We create tailored experiences that reflect each company's identity, objectives and vision.

02How much does a wedding or event planner cost in Greece?+

The cost depends on the type of event, location, guest count, planning requirements and level of service. SANDI MOMENTS offers personalised proposals based on each client's needs, whether planning a destination wedding, a corporate event or an exclusive private celebration.

03How can I book SANDI MOMENTS for my wedding or event in Greece?+

Getting started is easy. Contact SANDI MOMENTS through our website and share your preferred date, location, estimated number of guests and the type of event you are planning. We will discuss your vision, understand your requirements and guide you towards the right planning service for your occasion.
