Full Wedding Planning Package

For couples who want a dedicated professional to oversee every aspect of their wedding, from the initial vision to the final celebration.

Full Planning typically begins 8–12+ months before the wedding, depending on the destination and requirements.

I Pre-Wedding Services

  • Wedding planning timeline and checklist
  • Establishing priorities and budget management
  • Ceremony and reception venue scouting
  • Vendor management
  • Wedding concept, design coordination and styling, floral etc.
  • Save-the-dates, invitations and printed materials coordination
  • Guest RSVP tracking
  • Accommodation and transportation arrangements
  • Guest logistics
  • Marriage documentation guidance
  • Etiquette information
  • Unlimited planning meetings and communication within business hours

II Wedding Day

  • Full on-site coordination team
  • Ceremony processional direction
  • Ceremony and reception setup coordination
  • Guest book, place cards and favours arrangement
  • Coordination of flowers, bouquets and boutonnieres (decoration)
  • Main point of contact for all vendors
  • Coordination of final vendor payments and gratuities, provided by the client
  • Emergency kit
  • Coordination of event breakdown and load-out

Additional coordination can be arranged for Welcome Parties, Rehearsal Dinners, Sunday Brunches and other wedding weekend celebrations.

PricingFrom €6,500
