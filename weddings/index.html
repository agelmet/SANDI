FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything couples, companies and private clients ask us most often — from timings and seasons to destination weddings and private dining.

01What wedding planning services does SANDI EVENTS offer in Greece?+

SANDI EVENTS offers Full Wedding Planning, Partial Wedding Planning and Day-of Coordination services in Athens and throughout Greece. From complete wedding organisation to professional coordination on your special day, we create personalised experiences tailored to your vision, style and needs.

02How far in advance should I book a wedding planner in Greece?+

We recommend booking your wedding planner 12–18 months before your wedding, especially for popular destinations such as Koufonisia, Kea, Kythnos, Santorini, Mykonos and the Greek islands. Early planning allows sufficient time for venue selection, supplier bookings and all the details that make your wedding unforgettable.

03What is the difference between Full Wedding Planning, Partial Planning and Day-of Coordination?+

Full Wedding Planning covers every stage of your wedding, from the initial concept to the final execution. Partial Planning is ideal for couples who have already organised some elements and need professional support with the remaining arrangements. Day-of Coordination focuses on managing suppliers, timelines and logistics on the wedding day, allowing you to enjoy your celebration stress-free.

04What is the best time of year to get married in Greece?+

The most popular wedding season in Greece is from May to October. June, September and early October are particularly popular for their pleasant weather and beautiful outdoor settings. July and August are ideal for summer island celebrations, while spring and autumn offer wonderful alternatives for a more relaxed wedding experience.
