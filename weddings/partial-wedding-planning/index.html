Partial Wedding Planning Package

For couples who have already started planning their wedding and need professional guidance, organisation and coordination to complete the remaining details.

Typically begins 2–6 months before the wedding, depending on the progress of the planning.

I Pre-Wedding Services

  • Three pre-planning consultations
  • Assistance with budget management
  • Vendor recommendations upon request
  • Vendor matching assistance
  • Wedding preparation checklist
  • Venue site walk
  • Floor plan design
  • Wedding day timeline
  • Vendor and wedding party coordination
  • Complete vendor information sheet
  • Final vendor confirmations
  • Ceremony rehearsal coordination

II Wedding Day

  • On-site coordination team
  • Ceremony processional direction
  • Cueing the couple, family and wedding party
  • Wedding day agenda and timeline management
  • Setup coordination for ceremony and reception
  • Setup of wedding accessories
  • Coordination of bouquets and boutonnieres
  • Main point of contact for vendors
  • Assistance to the wedding party, family and guests
  • Additional assistant coordinator if required
  • Emergency kit
  • Management of unexpected situations
  • Up to 8–10 hours of on-site coordination
