FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything couples, companies and private clients ask us most often — from timings and seasons to destination weddings and private dining.

01What wedding planning services does SANDI EVENTS offer in Greece?+

SANDI EVENTS offers Full Wedding Planning, Partial Wedding Planning and Day-of Coordination services in Athens and throughout Greece. From complete wedding organisation to professional coordination on your special day, we create personalised experiences tailored to your vision, style and needs.

02How far in advance should I book a wedding planner in Greece?+

We recommend booking your wedding planner 12–18 months before your wedding, especially for popular destinations such as Koufonisia, Kea, Kythnos, Santorini, Mykonos and the Greek islands. Early planning allows sufficient time for venue selection, supplier bookings and all the details that make your wedding unforgettable.

03What is the difference between Full Wedding Planning, Partial Planning and Day-of Coordination?+

Full Wedding Planning covers every stage of your wedding, from the initial concept to the final execution. Partial Planning is ideal for couples who have already organised some elements and need professional support with the remaining arrangements. Day-of Coordination focuses on managing suppliers, timelines and logistics on the wedding day, allowing you to enjoy your celebration stress-free.

04Can you organise destination weddings and civil or symbolic ceremonies in Greece?+

Absolutely! We organise destination weddings throughout Greece, including civil, symbolic and religious celebrations. Whether you dream of a seaside ceremony in Santorini, an elegant wedding in Athens or an intimate celebration on a Greek island, we help coordinate the experience according to your preferences and the applicable legal requirements.

05What is the best time of year to get married in Greece?+

The most popular wedding season in Greece is from May to October. June, September and early October are particularly popular for their pleasant weather and beautiful outdoor settings. July and August are ideal for summer island celebrations, while spring and autumn offer wonderful alternatives for a more relaxed wedding experience.

06What types of corporate events does SANDI EVENTS organise in Athens and Greece?+

SANDI EVENTS provides corporate event planning services for conferences, gala dinners, award ceremonies, business meetings, product launches, brand activations, networking events, company celebrations and incentive events. We create tailored experiences that reflect each company's identity, objectives and vision.

07Do you organise private dining and exclusive private events in Greece?+

Yes! We organise bespoke private dining experiences, private dinners, birthday celebrations, anniversaries, engagement parties, bachelorette parties and other exclusive gatherings. From elegant tablescapes and personalised menus to venue selection and event coordination, every detail is designed around your occasion.

08How much does a wedding or event planner cost in Greece?+

The cost depends on the type of event, location, guest count, planning requirements and level of service. SANDI EVENTS offers personalised proposals based on each client's needs, whether planning a destination wedding, a corporate event or an exclusive private celebration.

09Can you organise a wedding or event in Greece if I live abroad?+

Absolutely! We work with international couples, businesses and private clients planning events in Greece. Through online consultations, remote planning and professional local coordination, we make the entire process convenient and seamless, wherever you are based.

10How can I book SANDI EVENTS for my wedding or event in Greece?+

Getting started is easy. Contact SANDI EVENTS through our website and share your preferred date, location, estimated number of guests and the type of event you are planning. We will discuss your vision, understand your requirements and guide you towards the right planning service for your occasion.
