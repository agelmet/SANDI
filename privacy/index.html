Privacy & Cookies

  1. Home
  2. Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is SANDI EVENTS (Kyriaki Kalou), based in Athens, Greece. You can contact us through the contact form on this website.

2. What we collect and why

When you send a request through the “Plan Your Event” form, we receive the details you choose to give us (name, e-mail, phone, event type, date, location, guests, budget, services and your message). We use them only to reply to your request and to prepare a proposal. The legal basis is your request and your consent.

3. Service providers

The website is served by a hosting provider and form requests are delivered to us by an e-mail delivery service. They process data only on our behalf. We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

4. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Fonts and images are served from the website itself, so nothing from other companies loads when you visit. If we add statistics in the future, they will load only after you agree.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your request for as long as needed to reply and, if we work together, for as long as the law requires for our agreement and invoices.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use and to receive a copy of it. You can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr).
